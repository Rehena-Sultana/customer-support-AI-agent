# Company Support FAQs

## 1. How do I reset my password?
Use the Forgot Password link on the sign-in page. We will send a password-reset link to the verified email address on your account. Never share your password or one-time code with support staff.

## 2. How can I track a support ticket?
Sign in to your account and provide your ticket number. Support can show the current status of tickets associated with your authenticated account. Common statuses are open, in_progress, and resolved.

## 3. How do I create a new support ticket?
Sign in to your account and describe the issue you are encountering. If the assistant cannot resolve the issue directly or if you ask for ongoing investigation, a ticket will be opened automatically for you, and a ticket ID will be provided.

## 4. What do the different ticket statuses mean?
- **open**: Your request has been received and is queued for initial review.
- **in_progress**: A support team member is actively working on your case.
- **resolved**: The solution or final response has been provided. If you still need help, you can reply to reopen the discussion.

## 5. How do I contact a human support representative?
Ask to speak to a human representative, live agent, or manager. You can also request escalation if you feel your problem is too complex or sensitive for the automated agent. The system records an escalation summary and conversation context for human review.

## 6. How long does human escalation review take?
Escalations are placed into a high-priority review queue. During standard operating hours, an agent will review the handoff summary and contact you within 1 to 2 business hours.

## 7. What should I include when reporting a technical issue?
Describe what you were trying to do, the exact error message, when the issue started, and the device or browser you use. Do not include passwords or payment card details.

## 8. Can the chatbot see my account details?
Only after account verification. The demo application uses a customer ID and demo PIN; a real deployment must use secure authentication and authorization. Unauthenticated chats cannot access account records.

## 9. Can I request a refund or billing adjustment through this chat?
The automated chat agent cannot authorize refunds or modify payment details directly. If you have a refund inquiry or billing dispute, please state your request and the assistant will route your case to an authorized human representative or billing specialist.

## 10. How do I change my registered email address or profile name?
For security, profile changes such as email or primary name updates require identity re-verification. You can request a profile update ticket through support, or visit your account settings page once authenticated.

## 11. How do I send voice or audio messages?
You can upload an audio file (e.g., WAV, MP3, M4A, OGG, or WEBM) using the audio uploader on the chat screen. The system will transcribe your audio message and process it just like a typed inquiry.

## 12. What should I do if my account is locked?
Accounts are temporarily locked after multiple failed verification attempts to protect your security. Wait 15 minutes before attempting to sign in again, or reset your password using the verified email on file.

## 13. How is my sensitive and payment data protected?
Our systems adhere to strict privacy standards. Support representatives and chatbots will never ask for your full credit card number, CVV, or passwords. Please do not type sensitive payment numbers into the chat window.

## 14. What browsers and platforms are supported?
The platform supports all modern evergreen browsers, including Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, and Microsoft Edge on Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android.

## 15. What are the customer support operating hours?
Automated AI support and ticket creation are available 24/7. Human support representatives are available Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST, and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST.

## 16. How do I check for active system maintenance or outages?
You can ask support for current system status or visit our official status page. Any scheduled maintenance windows are announced at least 48 hours in advance.

## 17. How do I cancel or close an open support ticket?
If your issue is resolved before an agent contacts you, you can inform the chat assistant with your ticket ID to close it, or reply directly to the confirmation email stating that the matter is resolved.

## 18. Can I export my chat transcript or ticket history?
Yes. When signed in, all your past tickets and their statuses are visible directly in your account dashboard. You can also request a complete transcript of any chat session for your records.

## 19. What file formats and size limits apply to audio uploads?
The audio input accepts WAV, MP3, M4A, OGG, FLAC, and WEBM formats with a maximum file size of 25 MB per recording. Clear speech with minimal background noise provides the highest transcription accuracy.

## 20. How do I enable two-factor authentication (2FA)?
Go to your Account Security settings and choose 'Enable Two-Factor Authentication'. Scan the QR code using any standard authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator) and enter the 6-digit confirmation code.
