# Company Support FAQs

## How do I reset my password?
Use the Forgot Password link on the sign-in page. We will send a password-reset link to the verified email address on your account. Never share your password or one-time code with support staff.

## How can I track a support ticket?
Sign in to your account and provide your ticket number. Support can show the current status of tickets associated with your authenticated account. Common statuses are open, in_progress, and resolved.

## How do I contact a human support representative?
Ask to speak to a human representative, live agent, or manager. The system records an escalation summary and conversation context for human review.

## What should I include when reporting a technical issue?
Describe what you were trying to do, the error message, when the issue started, and the device or browser you use. Do not include passwords or payment card details.

## Can the chatbot see my account details?
Only after account verification. The demo application uses a customer ID and demo PIN; a real deployment must use secure authentication and authorization.
