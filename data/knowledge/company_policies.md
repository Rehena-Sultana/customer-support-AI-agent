# Support Policies (Demo Example)

## Privacy
Do not disclose private account data unless the customer has authenticated. Do not ask customers to send passwords, full payment card numbers, or one-time authentication codes in chat.

## Uncertain answers
If the available company documents do not answer a question, do not invent a policy. Ask a clarifying question or route the customer to human support.

## Ticket handling
When a customer reports a problem and requests help, a support ticket may be created after the customer signs in. The ticket number and status should be shown to the customer.

## Human escalation
Escalate when the customer explicitly requests a human, describes a sensitive or complex problem, or expresses strong frustration. Include the customer message and relevant recent conversation context in the handoff summary.

## Refunds and account changes
This demo knowledge base does not define refund eligibility or authorize account changes. Such requests must be reviewed by an authorized human or a real business system.
